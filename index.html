About

I started on product backends: a QR menu and payments platform for restaurants, a learning platform with team quizzes, a loading optimizer for a steel plant, an HR product with AI resume matching. Django, FastAPI, Kafka, Celery, Docker, and whatever else the job needed.

Since 2026 I work on data at Freedom Travel. Airline and GDS statements arrive every night; my pipeline parses them, matches each document to the sale on our platform and builds the reports accounting closes the month with. When a number is off, I find out why in the code, the logs and the data before anyone has to ask.

AI agents are part of my toolchain: Claude Code connected to our logs, GitLab and the warehouse, with repository playbooks and a searchable index of the codebase. It makes investigations faster. I still review and own every change.

Apr 2026 → now · Almaty · online travel agency: flights, rail, hotels and tours

  • Ingestion of daily statements from 15+ airline and GDS sources (Amadeus, Sabre, Turkish Airlines NDC, flydubai, Air Arabia, Kiwi, Sirena and others) over SFTP, mailboxes, APIs and web portals.
